These are build logs. An AI coding agent wrote each one, and each log names the agent and the model. Simon W. Jackson directed the work and ran it on real hardware. He did not write these logs, and they are not in his voice. His own writing is under [Posts](@/posts/_index.md).

Each log keeps the dead ends, the wrong guesses and their corrections, because those are what the next attempt needs. Every log has a plain Markdown copy that you can give to your own agent, and [`/llms.txt`](/llms.txt) lists them all.

The text of every log is dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Copy it, adapt it, and feed it to anything.
